Privacy Policy

Effective: 20 November 2026

Short version: FocusMask does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

What the extension stores, and where

  • Preferences (selected sound, volume, auto-pause, screen-lock pause and media-key settings, break-reminder settings, whether you have seen the welcome screen) are saved with Chrome’s chrome.storage.sync. If you use Chrome Sync, Google syncs these between your own signed-in browsers under Google’s privacy policy. FocusMask’s developer never receives them.
  • Playback state (playing/paused, break and sleep timers) is kept in chrome.storage.session and is cleared when Chrome closes.
  • Audio files you upload are stored only in the extension’s local IndexedDB on your device. They are never uploaded or synced anywhere. Deleting a sound, or removing the extension, deletes them.

Browser tab audio

For the optional auto-pause feature, FocusMask asks Chrome whether any tab is currently making sound (Chrome’s audible and muted flags). It does not request the tabs permission and cannot read the address, title or content of your tabs. This check happens locally and nothing about it is stored or sent anywhere.

Headphone and media keys

When media-key control is on, FocusMask tells your operating system’s media controls that it is playing (the title shown is the sound’s name), so your headphone button or play/pause key can control it. No information is collected or sent anywhere.

Network access

FocusMask makes no network requests. All sounds, including the break chimes, are generated on your device, and it works offline.

Permissions

  • offscreen: plays audio in a hidden page so sound continues after the popup closes.
  • storage: saves your preferences.
  • unlimitedStorage: lets you keep uploaded audio files locally.
  • alarms: schedules optional break reminders and the sleep timer.
  • notifications: shows the optional break reminder.
  • idle: notices when your screen is locked so the sound can pause until you unlock it. Only the locked/unlocked state is used, and it is not stored or sent anywhere.

This website

focusmask.bhaggat.in is a static site with no analytics, cookies or third-party scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under GitHub’s own privacy statement.

Children

FocusMask does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
