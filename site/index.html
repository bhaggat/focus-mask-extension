How it works

Set it once. Forget it’s there.

No setup, no account. It keeps playing after you close the popup and gets out of the way on its own.

  1. Pick a sound

    Click the toolbar icon and choose brown noise, rain, ocean or any of the eight built-ins. Or add your own audio.

  2. Get to work

    Close the popup. The sound keeps going in the background and drowns out chatter, traffic and the open office.

  3. Join the call

    When a meeting or video starts in another tab, FocusMask fades out. When it ends, the sound fades back in.

A closer look

Small popup. Thoughtful settings.

Private by design

Your focus is nobody else’s business.

No accounts, no analytics, no servers. FocusMask runs entirely inside your browser.

No data collected

Your preferences stay in Chrome’s own storage. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Works offline

Every sound is generated locally. FocusMask makes no network requests.

Minimal permissions

It can’t read your tabs. It only checks whether some tab is making sound.

Honest note: Chrome extensions can only see browser tabs. FocusMask can’t detect desktop apps, phone calls or people talking nearby. Use the toolbar button or shortcut for those. Read the privacy policy.

FAQ

Good questions

Why doesn’t it pause for my Zoom or Teams desktop app?

Extensions can only see browser tabs. If you use a desktop app, press Alt+Shift+M or click the toolbar button when the call starts.

Which sound should I use?

For voices and conversations, brown noise masks best. Pink noise is a good all-rounder. Nature sounds mask less but are nicer for long sessions.

Does the sound keep playing if I close the popup?

Yes. Playback runs in the background until you pause it or close Chrome.

Is it really free?

Yes. No trial, no account, no ads, and no data collection.
