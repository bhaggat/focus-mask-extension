Block out the noise. Step aside for the meeting.

FocusMask puts brown, pink and white noise plus calm nature sounds in your Chrome toolbar. When another tab starts playing audio, it fades out on its own, and it fades back in when that tab goes quiet.

See how it works

Free · Works offline · No account · No tracking

Everything you need to focus, nothing you don’t

One click to play. It keeps going after you close the popup, and it knows when to get out of the way.

Auto-pause for meetings and videos

Google Meet, Zoom or Teams in the browser, YouTube, Spotify Web: when a tab makes sound, FocusMask fades out. Muted tabs are ignored.

8 built-in sounds

Brown, pink and white noise, rain, ocean, stream, fireplace and forest. All generated on your device, with no downloads.

Your own sounds

Add MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC or M4A files. They loop seamlessly and stay on your device.

Gentle break reminders

Optional. A soft chime and a 5-second countdown before the sound pauses for your break and before it comes back.

Sleep timer

Fade out after 15 minutes, 2 hours, or any length you choose, up to 12 hours.

Hands-free control

Alt+Shift+M, your headphone button or media keys play and pause. It also pauses while your screen is locked.

A closer look

A small popup for everyday use, and a settings page for the rest.

No data collected

No accounts, analytics or servers. Your preferences stay in Chrome’s own storage.

Works offline

Every sound is generated locally. FocusMask makes no network requests.

Minimal permissions

It can’t read your tabs. It only checks whether some tab is making sound.

Honest note: Chrome extensions can only see browser tabs. FocusMask can’t detect desktop apps, phone calls or people talking nearby. Use the toolbar button or shortcut for those.
