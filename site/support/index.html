Frequently asked questions

Why doesn’t it pause for my Zoom or Teams call?

It can only see browser tabs. If you use the desktop app, press Alt+Shift+M or click the toolbar button when a call starts.

Why did it pause when nothing important was playing?

Any audible tab counts, including ads, notification chimes longer than a second or two, and autoplaying videos. Mute that tab (right-click its tab, then Mute site) and FocusMask will ignore it, or turn auto-pause off.

Which sound should I use?

For voices and conversations, brown noise masks best. Pink noise is a good all-rounder. Nature sounds mask less but are nicer for long sessions.

How do break reminders work?

Turn them on in Settings, then choose how often and how long. Five seconds before a break, a soft falling chime plays and the toolbar badge counts down. Then the sound pauses. Five seconds before the break ends, a rising chime plays and the sound comes back. The popup lets you change the break length or restart the timer, and “End break & resume” ends a break early.

Can it stop on its own after a while?

Yes. Use the Sleep timer in the popup: 15 min, 30 min, 1 h, 2 h, or Custom for anything up to 12 hours. When it runs out the sound fades out and stays paused.

Can I use my own audio?

Yes. Open Settings, then My sounds, and drop in MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC or M4A files up to 50 MB each. They stay on your device. Click the pencil to rename one.

My headphone button controls FocusMask instead of my music.

FocusMask only takes the media keys while no other tab is playing audio. To turn this off, open Settings, then Controls, and switch off Headphone & media keys.

Does the sound keep playing if I close the popup?

Yes. Playback runs in the background until you pause it or close Chrome.

Does FocusMask collect any data?

No. See the privacy policy.

You’ll usually get a reply within a few days.
